V2 passed format, ordinary-library compilation, test compilation, exact listing and all 53 models, then failed warnings-denied Clippy (`manual_inspect`); docs/MSRV were not run. The snapshots are standalone, dependency-free crates. They are not active profiler integration, a native entry/return path, a timestamp/PMU result, a software/preemption clock, full A5, or evidence for claims 1–4. Elapsed times are operational diagnostics, not speed measurements.
