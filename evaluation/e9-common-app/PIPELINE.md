The build, observation, production and candidate-run recipes are source-reviewed
and have not been run for this compiled corpus. A static selection retains every
verified head and every
selected site, including sites with zero dynamic visits. An emitted candidate,
or a zero exit from a structural comparator, does not establish application
behavior or architectural equivalence. Ordinary rewritten outputs, complete
ordered hook journals, full state, faults, loader behavior and unwind require
their own execution records.

The original observer is a separate single-step protocol. It executes the real
loader, temporarily installs and completely restores one breakpoint, then
checks the kernel's actual instruction visits, calls, returns, heap destinations
and guarded boundaries. Independent execs have fresh heap, stack and TLS
addresses. These observations do not supply identical per-head architectural
states for a cross-run equivalence comparison. A future controlled replay must
admit every head's complete state and prove disjoint application and
instrumentation memory before using the existing strict comparator.

Preserve failed and unrun rows and the original limits. Do not reduce a
selection after a refusal, enlarge a budget after exhaustion, or count a trap
as a trampoline. Raw binaries, streams, maps and captures remain ignored;
publish sanitized CSV/JSON with the exact revision, hardware and command when
each execution closes. These records support parts of claim 4. Full A3 and
claims 1–3 remain open.
