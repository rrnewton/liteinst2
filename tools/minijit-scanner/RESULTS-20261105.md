## Correctness oracle (the gate)

At every iced-x86 instruction boundary in each `.text`, the
scanner length and movable bit were compared with iced plus
`liteinst-jit` admission (`decode_one` class for
relative/RIP/intercept facts and `decode_supported` for
allowlisted Ordinary admission). A false movable is the
non-conservative direction and blocks the result.

| corpus | boundaries | skipped | length disagreements | false movable | missed movable | fallback insn fraction | scanner movable insns | scanner movable bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| libc | 366,364 | 0 | **0** | **0** | 308 | 35.54% | 64.46% | 59.80% |
| libLLVM | 15,822,602 | 0 | **0** | **0** | 36 | 29.47% | 70.53% | 70.83% |

Site counts from the oracle sweep: libc 68,066 direct-relative,
11,548 RIP-relative, 512 intercept; libLLVM 2,930,545
direct-relative, 294,143 RIP-relative, 26 intercept. Missed
movable cases are conservative cost only (oracle movable,
scanner fallback), not correctness failures.

## Speed (run 1 medians)

| corpus | scanner | iced stream | supported stream | fast end-to-end | full end-to-end |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| libc | 182.3 MB/s, 43.81 Minsn/s | 182.8 MB/s, 43.92 | 72.0 MB/s, 17.31 | 114.5 MB/s, 27.52 | 35.4 MB/s, 8.51 |
| libLLVM | 200.8 MB/s, 50.77 Minsn/s | 200.8 MB/s, 50.78 | 71.0 MB/s, 17.95 | 132.8 MB/s, 33.57 | 33.8 MB/s, 8.54 |

Run 2 medians agree in shape: scanner 186.1 / 200.3 MB/s, iced
186.7 / 199.1 MB/s, supported stream 72.9 / 72.4 MB/s, fast
end-to-end 112.8 / 135.5 MB/s, full end-to-end 34.0 / 34.3 MB/s.

Reading: scanner-only throughput is at parity with same-run
iced decode-only (0.997× on libc, 1.000× on libLLVM) and
brackets the earlier S3 iced figure of 191 MB/s. The measured
gain is end-to-end: copying scanner-movable runs with memcpy
and full-decoding only the rest is 1.59× (libc) and 1.87×
(libLLVM) the sequential S3-A supported-decoder stream, and
3.23× / 3.93× the one-shot full-decode-and-copy baseline in
this driver. The fast path therefore leaves roughly 60–71% of
real `.text` bytes in movable runs untouched by full decode,
under the known-boundaries constraint above.
